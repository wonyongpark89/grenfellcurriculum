CPD Week 2

By the end of Week 2, you will be able to:

1

Understand the events of the night of the Grenfell Tower fire using evidence and survivors’ accounts.

2

Explain key considerations and areas of focus when teaching about Grenfell.

3

Select appropriate and subject-relevant content for teaching about Grenfell.
