Survivors’ Accounts

CPD Week 2

While official advice told residents to “stay put,” many Grenfell survivors took matters into their own hands, banging on doors, alerting neighbours, and guiding people out of the building. Their actions saved lives.

Miguel Alves, 13th Floor

Miguel told his children to leave the building, then knocked on every door on his floor, despite worrying his neighbours might be annoyed to be woken.

“It was my duty to help my neighbours, because they are my family; the people that live on my floor is like my family.”

Shahid Ahmed, 18th Floor

Shahid decided to leave after his kitchen window exploded. Even as he fled down the single stairwell, firefighters told him to go back up.

“I remember saying to one of them: ‘There’s a fire upstairs,’ and they told me to go back up. They had masks on. I couldn’t see their faces.”

Source: The Guardian, Grenfell Tower survivors tell how they ignored advice to stay put[R033]
