Grenfell Tower: A Timeline of Failures

CPD Week 1

This interactive timeline asks you to trace key decisions and missed warnings before, during and after the Grenfell Tower fire of 14 June 2017, in which 72 people lost their lives.

As you work through it, consider not only what happened, but where responsibility, communication and accountability broke down.
